Acme Consulting - Internal Policy Handbook (Sample)

Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Remote work requests must be
approved by a direct manager at least 48 hours in advance. Core collaboration
hours are 10am-3pm local time, during which employees should be reachable.

Expense Reimbursement
Business expenses under $75 can be submitted with a receipt via the expense
portal and are typically reimbursed within 5 business days. Expenses over $75
require pre-approval from a department head before the purchase is made.

Client Onboarding Process
New client engagements begin with a discovery call, followed by a written
scope-of-work document within 5 business days. All engagements require a
signed agreement before any billable work begins. The standard onboarding
checklist includes: kickoff call, access provisioning, and a 30-day check-in.

Vacation Policy
Full-time employees accrue 15 vacation days per year, accrued monthly.
Unused vacation days roll over up to a maximum of 5 days into the next year.
Vacation requests should be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance when possible.
