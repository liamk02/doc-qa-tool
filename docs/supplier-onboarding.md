# Nordvik Trading AB - Supplier Onboarding and Quality Standards
Procurement procedure PRO-07. Owner: Head of Procurement.

## 1. Qualification
A prospective supplier completes the qualification pack before any trial order: company registration, F-skatt certificate, proof of liability insurance to a minimum of 10 million SEK, and a food safety certification where applicable.

Procurement checks the registration against Bolagsverket and runs a credit report. Suppliers with a credit score below the internal threshold may still be approved, but only on prepayment terms and with sign-off from the finance director.

## 2. Trial Period
Every new supplier runs a 90-day trial covering a minimum of six deliveries. During the trial, all inbound consignments are subject to enhanced inspection rather than the standard sampling regime.

The trial is assessed against three measures: on-time delivery of at least 95 percent, order accuracy of at least 98 percent, and zero critical quality failures. A critical failure is any temperature breach on chilled goods, any incorrect allergen labelling, or any delivery of expired stock.

A single critical failure ends the trial immediately. Missing the on-time or accuracy targets triggers a review rather than automatic termination, and the trial may be extended once by 60 days.

## 3. Payment Terms to Suppliers
Nordvik's standard terms to suppliers are 30 days from invoice. New suppliers are paid at 14 days for the duration of the trial period, which is a deliberate concession to secure good terms on price.

Requests from suppliers for terms shorter than 30 days after the trial require approval from the finance director and are granted only where a genuine price advantage is documented.

Nordvik does not agree to terms longer than 45 days, as this has historically been used to disguise supply instability.

## 4. Labelling and Documentation
Every consignment must carry a delivery note stating batch numbers and best-before dates. Consignments arriving without batch traceability are quarantined and are not put away until the documentation is produced.

Allergen information must match the product specification held on file exactly. Any change to a product's formulation, packaging or country of origin must be notified in writing 30 days before the first affected delivery. Unnotified formulation changes are treated as a critical quality failure.

## 5. Shelf Life on Arrival
Chilled goods must arrive with at least 75 percent of their total shelf life remaining. Ambient goods must arrive with at least 6 months remaining, or 60 percent of total shelf life, whichever is shorter.

Stock arriving short-dated is rejected at the door. Where it has already been accepted in error, the supplier is charged the difference between the agreed price and the realisable value of the short-dated stock.

## 6. Ongoing Review
Approved suppliers are reviewed every six months against the same three measures used in the trial. Suppliers falling below target for two consecutive reviews are placed on a 90-day improvement plan with a named contact on both sides.

Failure to complete an improvement plan results in delisting. A delisted supplier may reapply after twelve months and must complete the full qualification and trial process again.

## 7. Single-Source Risk
Procurement maintains a register of lines where Nordvik has only one qualified supplier. Any line representing more than 3 percent of annual turnover must have a second qualified source, or a documented board-approved exception with a stated review date.
