# Nordvik Trading AB - Employee Handbook
Version 4.2, revised January 2026. Applies to all permanent and fixed-term staff.

## 1. Working Hours and Remote Work
Standard working time is 40 hours per week. Core collaboration hours are 09:30 to 15:00 CET, during which all staff are expected to be reachable on Slack or by phone.

Office-based staff may work remotely up to three days per week. Warehouse and logistics staff are excluded from remote work for operational reasons. Remote days must be logged in Planday by Friday of the preceding week. A manager may refuse a remote day if it conflicts with a scheduled stock count, an audit, or a client visit.

Staff working remotely from outside Sweden for more than 14 consecutive days must notify HR in advance, because this can create tax and social insurance obligations in the host country. Requests for periods longer than 30 days require written approval from both the department head and the finance director.

## 2. Expenses and Reimbursement
Expenses under 800 SEK may be submitted directly through the expense portal with a photographed receipt. These are normally paid with the following month's salary run, which closes on the 20th.

Expenses of 800 SEK or more require pre-approval from a department head before the money is spent. Submitting a large expense after the fact does not guarantee reimbursement, and repeated instances are treated as a performance matter.

Client entertainment is reimbursable up to 500 SEK per person per occasion, and the names of attending clients must be recorded on the claim for tax purposes. Alcohol is reimbursable only where it accompanies a meal.

Mileage in a private vehicle is reimbursed at the Skatteverket standard rate. Parking and congestion charges are reimbursable with a receipt. Traffic fines are never reimbursable, regardless of circumstances.

## 3. Annual Leave
Full-time employees accrue 25 days of paid annual leave per year, accruing monthly from the start date. Employees joining mid-year accrue pro rata.

Leave requests should be submitted at least three weeks in advance. Requests covering weeks 28 to 32, the main Swedish holiday period, must be submitted before 31 March, and where requests conflict the tie is broken by length of service.

A maximum of five unused days may be carried into the following year, and carried days expire on 31 March. Days beyond that limit are forfeited and are not paid out, except on termination of employment where all accrued untaken leave is paid at the final salary rate.

## 4. Sick Leave
Absence must be reported to your manager before 09:00 on the first day. The first day of any sickness period is a qualifying day (karensdag) and is unpaid. From day two through day fourteen, the company pays sick pay at 80 percent of normal salary.

A doctor's certificate is required from the eighth consecutive day. From day fifteen, Försäkringskassan takes over payment and HR files the notification.

Care of a sick child (VAB) follows Försäkringskassan rules and should be reported to your manager the same way as personal sickness.

## 5. Equipment and Company Property
Each office employee is issued a laptop and a phone. Equipment remains company property and must be returned on the final day of employment. Loss or theft must be reported to IT within 24 hours, and separately to the police where theft is suspected.

Reasonable personal use of company devices is permitted. Installing unlicensed software, or storing company data on personal cloud accounts, is not.

## 6. Notice Periods
During the six-month probationary period, either party may terminate with 14 days notice. After probation, notice is one month for employees with less than two years of service, two months from two years, and three months from six years.

Employees are expected to hand over active client accounts in writing before their final day.
