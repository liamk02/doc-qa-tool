# Nordvik Trading AB - Standard Client Service Agreement
Template MSA-2026-01. Governs all wholesale supply relationships unless a signed variation exists.

## 1. Scope and Onboarding
A client relationship begins with a discovery call, after which Nordvik issues a written scope-of-supply document within five business days. No goods are dispatched and no work is billable until the client has returned a signed agreement and passed a credit check.

New clients are placed on prepayment terms for their first three orders. Credit terms are reviewed after the third completed order.

## 2. Pricing and Payment Terms
List prices are held for the duration of a quarter and may be revised with 30 days written notice. Volume tiers apply automatically once a client's rolling twelve-month spend crosses a threshold, and tier changes take effect from the following month.

Standard payment terms are 30 days from invoice date. Clients with more than 24 months of unbroken trading history may apply for 45-day terms, subject to approval by the finance director.

Late payment incurs interest at the Riksbank reference rate plus eight percentage points, applied from the day after the due date. Accounts more than 21 days overdue are placed on dispatch hold, and no further orders ship until the balance clears. Reinstatement is automatic once payment is received and requires no new credit check unless the account has been on hold more than twice in a twelve-month period.

## 3. Delivery and Order Cutoffs
Orders received before 14:00 CET on a business day are picked the same day for next-day delivery within Stockholm, Uppsala and Södertälje. Orders after that cutoff move to the following business day.

Deliveries outside those zones are quoted individually and typically run two to three business days. Nordvik does not guarantee delivery times during weeks 28 to 32 or between 20 December and 6 January, when reduced carrier capacity applies.

## 4. Damaged Goods, Shortages and Returns
Shortages and visible transit damage must be noted on the carrier's proof of delivery at the point of receipt and reported to Nordvik within 48 hours, with photographs. Claims raised after 48 hours without a delivery note annotation are not accepted, because the carrier's own liability window has closed.

Latent defects not visible on delivery may be reported up to 30 days from the delivery date. Nordvik will at its option replace the goods or issue a credit note.

Goods correctly supplied but no longer wanted may be returned within 14 days provided they are unopened, in original packaging and in resalable condition. A restocking fee of 15 percent applies. Chilled and short-dated lines are excluded from discretionary returns entirely.

Returns require a Return Authorisation number issued by customer service before the goods are sent back. Unauthorised returns are refused at the door.

## 5. Service Levels
Nordvik targets a 98 percent order fill rate measured monthly across all lines. Where the fill rate falls below 95 percent in a calendar month, affected clients are entitled to a two percent credit on that month's invoiced value, applied automatically without the need to claim.

Customer service responds to written enquiries within one business day. Escalations reaching the account manager are acknowledged within four working hours.

## 6. Liability
Nordvik's total liability under this agreement in any twelve-month period is capped at the value invoiced to that client over the preceding twelve months. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, including loss of profit or reputational damage.

Nothing in this clause limits liability for death, personal injury, or fraud.

## 7. Term and Termination
The agreement runs for an initial twelve months and renews automatically for successive twelve-month periods unless terminated.

Either party may terminate at the end of a term by giving 60 days written notice. Nordvik may terminate immediately where an account is more than 60 days overdue, where the client enters insolvency proceedings, or on a material breach that is not remedied within 14 days of written notice.

On termination, all outstanding invoices become payable immediately regardless of their original due date.
